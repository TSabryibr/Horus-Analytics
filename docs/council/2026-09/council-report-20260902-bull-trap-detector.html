Detailed Advisor Submissions

1. The Contrarian — The Long-Only EGX Market & Retest Traps Expand ▾

The EGX is a long-only market where shorting is heavily restricted. Labeling Bull Traps as 'SELL/SHORT NOW' is confusing because users cannot open short positions.

Furthermore, a 1-bar close back below resistance often occurs during normal, healthy breakout retests. Declaring a bull trap on Day 2 of a breakout can trigger premature liquidations right before a powerful continuation leg.

2. The First Principles Thinker — Unfilled Liquidity Hunts (Wyckoff UTAD & Springs) Expand ▾

A trap is an unfilled liquidity hunt where smart money sweeps stop-orders before reversing. In a long-only market, Bear Traps (Springs) are Grade-A offensive buying opportunities, while Bull Traps (Upthrusts) are defensive capital preservation alerts.

Reframe the tab around this asymmetry: Bear Traps are the offensive engine; Bull Traps are the risk shield.

3. The Expansionist — Live Portfolio Cross-Audit & Wyckoff Spring Scores Expand ▾

Cross-reference traps against active portfolio holdings in TreasuryLedger. If an active holding triggers a Bull Trap, fire a high-priority defensive trim alert.

Introduce a 100-point Wyckoff Spring score combining volume surge, fakeout depth, and close position relative to candle range.

4. The Outsider — Clearer Button Actions & Visual Chart Verifications Expand ▾

The Crimson vs Emerald two-column layout is visually clear and immediately understandable.

However, button labels on Bull Traps should say 'Stage Defensive Exit / Alert' rather than generic promotion, and a 1-click chart modal would allow instant inspection of the rejection wick.

5. The Executor — Candle Wick Geometry & Immediate Actionability Expand ▾

Dynamic ATR risk bounding in handlePromoteTrap is already solid. The three most immediate enhancements are:

  • Portfolio holding overlay badges.
  • Candle wick ratio validation in Svartalfheim.py to filter weak flat-body candles.
  • Explicit 'Promote Spring (Buy)' vs 'Stage Exit' button labels.
6. Anonymized Peer Review Highlights Expand ▾

  • Critical Blind Spot Caught: Filtering out penny-stock illiquidity traps where 3 retail orders can trigger a fake wick.
