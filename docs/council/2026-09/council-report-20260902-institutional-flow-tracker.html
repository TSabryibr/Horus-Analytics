Detailed Advisor Submissions

1. The Contrarian — Flaws in Raw 20-Day Daily OBV Expand ▾

On-Balance Volume assigns 100% of a session's volume to the close direction based on a single tick difference between today's close and yesterday's close. On the EGX, where closing prices are volume-weighted average prices (VWAP) or influenced by illiquid end-of-day crosses, raw daily OBV can produce false positives.

A single high-volume rebalancing day or block trade on Day 3 can tilt a 20-day linear polyfit slope positive even if the subsequent 17 days were completely dead. Furthermore, without checking whether the stock is near major historical resistance or trapped under a descending 200 EMA, buying raw 'OBV Accumulation' risks buying the bottom of a prolonged downtrend.

2. The First Principles Thinker — The Physical Trace of Stealth Accumulation Expand ▾

When an institution accumulates 100 million EGP in an EGX security (like COMI, HRHO, or EAST), they cannot buy market orders without causing 10% limit-up slippage. Therefore, by first principles, institutional execution is characterized by: (1) Time Persistence over 5–20 sessions, (2) Volume-to-Volatility Asymmetry (heavy volume on small price range expansion), and (3) Sector Clustering.

The current tab captures time persistence and sector clustering, but misses measuring Volume / Range Elasticity. Reframe the calculation from simple slope divergence to a composite Absorption Index = (OBV Slope * Liquidity Factor) / Normalized Volatility.

3. The Expansionist — Capital Flow in Native Millions & Sizing Multipliers Expand ▾

Institutional flow is one of the highest-alpha concepts in emerging markets. We should expand this from a single-tab scanner into a core intelligence engine:

  • Capital Flow in Native EGP Millions: Show Estimated Net Smart Money Flow (e.g. +42.5M EGP Net Inflow) instead of abstract slopes.
  • Multi-Timeframe Flow Matrix: Combine Daily (20D) and Weekly (10W) flow bias. When both align, assign a 5-Star Institutional Super-Setup badge.
  • Automated Sizing Multiplier: Allow execution algorithms to scale position size by 1.25x when backed by confirmed Whale Accumulation.
4. The Outsider — Cognitive Usability & Support Anchors Expand ▾

Landing on the page, the header telemetry (🟢 Accumulation Lead (+5) and Lead Sector: Real Estate) immediately grounds an operator within 2 seconds. However, candidate cards displaying OBV Slope: 0.1420 and Price Slope: -0.0015 create cognitive friction.

Replace raw slopes with intuitive percentile gauges (e.g. Stealth Inflow (88th Percentile)) and display the structural support anchor price so operators know their invalidation level before clicking Promote.
