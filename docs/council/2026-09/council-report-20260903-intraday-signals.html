The Contrarian

Focus: Downside, Phantom Breakouts, Lookahead Bias

"You are trading phantom signals created by mathematical illusions."

The single most dangerous line in your entire trading desk is LiveFeedManager.py:134. You send an institutional Telegram alert saying INTRADAY BREAKOUT on a single tick. In emerging markets with shallow books, market makers and retail algos frequently test liquidity above resistance with small orders. FATAL FLAW: A breakout without 5-minute candle closure confirmation and volume delta confirmation is not an intraday signal; it is an unforced donation to the market maker.

Furthermore, linear volume projection during the opening 45 minutes assumes trading continues at the 10:00 AM velocity all day. It never does. Morning breakouts are systematically over-reported, and afternoon breakouts are systematically under-reported.

The First Principles Thinker

Focus: Microstructure Truth, Signal-Data Alignment

"Horus has conflated two fundamentally different concepts under one name."

Let us rebuild this from ground truth:

  • Concept A: In-Session Daily Preview (Daily Pre-Close): You are trading a Daily Swing Strategy, but looking at the chart at 1:30 PM instead of 2:30 PM. The data required is: 90 days of EOD history + today's aggregated session bar with an empirical volume curve.
  • Concept B: Native Intraday Momentum Breakout: You are trading 5-minute or 15-minute price action. The data required is: discrete closed candles, session anchored VWAP, and multi-candle volume expansion.

Right now, Horus forces Concept A into DailyScanner without telling the trader it is merely an EOD projection, and butchers Concept B in LiveFeedManager by throwing away candle formation entirely. Separate them into two clean, distinct signal classes.
